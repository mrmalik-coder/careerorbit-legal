Privacy Policy

This draft explains the information processed by CareerOrbit's mobile application and related services. It must be reviewed and approved by the CareerOrbit owner and qualified Pakistani legal counsel before publication. Sajid Manzoor is an individual operator, not a company or registered legal entity. This draft must be reviewed before publication.

Operator: Sajid Manzoor (individual). Privacy requests: sajidmanzoor347@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, display name, authentication-provider information, and email-verification status. The app code includes email/password and Google sign-in flows; which providers are enabled in Firebase Authentication for a release is not verified. Authentication providers may process email addresses, passwords or provider identifiers, IP addresses, and user-agent information as described by Firebase.
  • Profile and career information: name, contact details, location, professional summary, skills, education, work history, preferences, certifications, languages, and profile links that a user enters.
  • CV information: CV content, templates, settings, and candidate-entered personal, education, experience, skills, and other sections.
  • Jobs and applications: employer and company details, job postings, application content and status, and related candidate or employer records.
  • Saved items and preferences: saved jobs or companies, notification preferences, and other settings used by the app.
  • Device notifications: on supported Android devices, notification permission and a push token, hashed token identifier, device identifier, platform, and app version may be stored in Firestore to deliver notifications.
  • Support and safety records: information a user submits in a support request, reports, or other platform interactions.

2. Documents, imports, and OCR

Job posters, shared files, PDFs, and copied LinkedIn profile text can be selected or provided for import. In the current mobile implementation, image text recognition uses on-device ML Kit; PDF pages are rendered on the device and recognized as images. Extracted text is shown for review. The import code does not upload source documents to Firebase Storage.

The app and operating system may use temporary local/cache files while processing. A fixed cleanup interval has not been verified. Users should avoid importing information they do not have permission to use.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, maintain profiles and CVs, create and manage job postings, support applications and employer workflows, provide requested notifications, process user-initiated imports, operate support and safety features, and maintain platform security and functionality.

4. How information is shared

  • Employers can receive information that candidates submit in applications to their jobs. Job listings and employer details may be shown to candidates.
  • A CV is not made public merely by creating it. If a candidate creates an active share link, CareerOrbit publishes a limited projection that includes the professional summary, work experience, education, skills, and certifications. Contact details are excluded from that projection. The owner can revoke a share link.
  • Google and Firebase provide authentication, database, and notification services. Google processes information when a user chooses Google sign-in. Android push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging where available and permitted by the user's device settings.
  • External application methods, including WhatsApp or external employer sites, are controlled by the user and their respective providers. Opening an external destination does not itself submit a CareerOrbit application.

5. Storage and security

CareerOrbit uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore in its current implementation. The inspected Firebase Staging project has a default Cloud Firestore Native database, Standard edition, in asia-south1 (Mumbai, India). This is a Staging fact, not confirmation of the location used by any release or Production environment. Firestore rules restrict access to account-owned data and use explicit access rules for public CV-share projections.

Firebase Authentication processes data in US data centers, as described in Google's Firebase privacy information. Google and Firebase may also process information internationally to provide their services. The Firebase project ID does not establish a location for every product. No Firebase Storage bucket or bucket location was verified. The reviewed mobile import code processes selected images and PDFs on-device and contains no upload of those source documents to Firebase Storage. No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK was found in the reviewed mobile dependency manifest.

No internet service can promise absolute security. This policy does not make a broader encryption or security guarantee than the implementation supports.

6. Retention and deletion

CareerOrbit retains information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, service operation, security, dispute handling, and applicable legal obligations. The detailed schedule, including backup cleanup and records that may need retention or anonymization, has not been confirmed. Users can delete individual CVs and revoke CV share links through supported app functions. Revoking a link prevents future access through that link but cannot recall copies already viewed or saved by others. A server-side account-deletion workflow exists in source, but deployment and end-to-end operation are not verified; it is not an available or verified in-app self-service capability. Related application, audit, wallet, and business records may require retention or anonymization decisions; those rules are pending owner and legal review.

To request access, correction, or deletion, email sajidmanzoor347@gmail.com. A request is not a promise of immediate or complete deletion; identity verification and applicable retention requirements may apply.

CareerOrbit is intended for people who are at least 16 years old. People under 16 must not create or use an account. The age threshold and children's privacy approach require review by qualified Pakistani legal counsel before publication.

Apart from the Staging Firestore location stated above, locations for other resources and any release environment have not been verified. The appropriate legal basis, required disclosures, and any cross-border transfer requirements must be assessed by qualified Pakistani legal counsel before publication.
